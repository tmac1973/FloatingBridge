# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First test release.

- **Pontoon** build piece on the hammer (Building, 4 Wood + 2 Resin, needs a workbench): a 2×2 wooden deck on two logs.
- Placement snaps to sea level plus a configurable deck height, and is refused over dry land or very shallow water.
- Always fully supported: floors, railings and walls can be built off it like off a piece on the ground.
- No wear from rain or from being under water.
- In-game test commands for development (`fb_test`, `fb_dump`).
